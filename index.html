Privacy Policy for Clockr

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

Clockr ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Clockr mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. Authentication is securely managed through Firebase Authentication.

b. Usage and Analytics Data

We may collect information about how you use the App, including feature usage, device information, operating system version, and crash reports. This information is collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

c. Time Tracking Data

The App stores time entries, session data, and related information necessary to provide time tracking functionality. This data may be stored locally on your device and synchronized to cloud storage using Firebase Firestore.

d. Subscription and Payment Information

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card or payment information. Payment processing is handled directly by Google.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App
  • Authenticate users and manage accounts
  • Sync data across devices
  • Process subscriptions
  • Improve performance and reliability
  • Analyze usage trends
  • Fix bugs and crashes

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Firebase services provided by Google. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share data with service providers including Google for the purposes of:

  • Cloud storage (Firebase)
  • Analytics and crash reporting
  • Subscription processing (Google Play)

These providers process data on our behalf.
